About

I'm a multimedia creative who takes raw concepts through to finished, professional work — from brand identity systems to broadcast show openers and product photography. Some of that has been under my own design business, Inkboard Design, on contract client by client; some under my own name in roles for other businesses, including directing a four-person design team through a full brand rollout.

I work across the whole stack of a brand's visual presence: identity systems, print and digital advertising, signage and environmental graphics, social content, photography, and motion design. Because I cover design, marketing, and production, a business gets a single point of contact instead of juggling three vendors — and a look that stays consistent everywhere it shows up.

Whether it's launching a campaign, refreshing an identity, building out event visuals, or keeping a content calendar full, I deliver work that is both visually striking and strategically sound — tied to a real goal, not just a pretty file.
