Two product mockup projects from coursework, both built around the
same skill: taking a brand's visual language and applying it to
real-world objects so a client can see what the finished product
looks like before it goes to manufacture.

## Skullcandy — Apparel

Skullcandy makes headphones and audio gear aimed at the action sports
market. The apparel side put the brand language on t-shirts and
v-necks — the full garment mockup shows the primary treatment; the
v-neck shows a cleaner cut with smaller mark placement.

## Skullcandy — Device Mockups

The device mockups put the brand on iPhones, laptops, and portable
speakers. Each one had to feel like the brand actually lived on the
device, not like a sticker slapped on top. The product sheets pull
everything together into presentation-ready layouts.

## Starbucks — Gift Card

The Starbucks project was a gift card design with full packaging
presentation. The card itself is the hero — a clean treatment of the
siren mark with a color palette that fits the brand's retail
environment. The product sheet shows the card alongside its packaging
and variant designs.

[[08-starbucks-mockup.png]]

[[grid:09-starbucks-card-sheet:wide]]

Both projects were built in Photoshop and Illustrator — the mockups
in Photoshop with layer comps for each device, and the brand art in
Illustrator so it could scale to any output size.
