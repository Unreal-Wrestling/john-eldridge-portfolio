The Vibrations series was made for the annual art exhibitions at Everett
Community College, where students showed work alongside visiting
artists. Each piece was printed large and hung as a standalone
statement.

## The process

Every piece in the series started as photography — my own shots, taken
around campus and in the surrounding area. I brought them into Photoshop
and built them up in layers: textures, gradients, hand-painted masks,
and composited elements that didn't belong to the original frames. The
goal was to make something that read as a painting from across the room
but revealed its photographic bones up close.

[[grid:01-vibrations-ii:xl]]

Vibrations I is the quietest piece in the series. It leans on
atmosphere — depth, light, negative space — rather than density.

[[02-vibrations-i.png]]

Vibrations III goes darker. The palette shifts, the textures get
heavier, and the composition pulls inward.

## Earlier work

Eldridge Final was the piece that started the direction the Vibrations
series eventually followed — a single composition that tested whether
Photoshop compositing could hold up as a gallery piece on its own.
