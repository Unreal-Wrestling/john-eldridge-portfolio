Alpine Tech was a brand identity project for Graph 213 at Everett
Community College. The brief was to create a complete identity for a
fictional alpine technology company — a brand that felt rugged and
precise at the same time, mountain-born but tech-forward. The
assignment required a primary mark, a color system, and application
mockups showing the identity in real-world use.

## The Mark

The concept was negative space. The mountain peak sits inside the
letter A, so the mark reads as both a wordmark and an icon at any
size. The geometry is clean enough to scale down to an app icon
without losing the form. I built it in Illustrator as pure vector
art — every angle deliberate, every curve a Bezier I placed by hand.

## Presentation

The presentation page pulls everything together — the logo lockup,
a blue and slate color palette chosen for its read of cold altitude
and technical precision, and application examples showing the mark
on a business card and in a brand context. The palette runs from
deep navy through ice blue, which keeps the alpine reference without
resorting to literal mountain imagery in every application.

[[02-presentation.png]]

The final files were delivered as vector Illustrator files with a
defined color palette, ready to scale to any output size.
