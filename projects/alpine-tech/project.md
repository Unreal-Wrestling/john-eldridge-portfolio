Alpine Tech was a brand identity project for Graph 213 at Everett
Community College. The brief was to create a logo for a fictional
alpine technology company — something that felt rugged and precise
at the same time.

## The Mark

The concept was negative space. The mountain peak sits inside the
letter A, so the mark reads as both a wordmark and an icon at any
size. The geometry is clean enough to scale down to an app icon
without losing the form.

[[01-logo.png]]

## Presentation

The presentation page pulls everything together — the logo, the
color palette, and application examples showing how the mark lives
on a business card and in a brand context.

[[02-presentation.png]]

The final files were delivered as vector Illustrator files with a
defined color palette, ready to scale to any output size.
