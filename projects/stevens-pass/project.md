Stevens Pass is a ski resort on the Cascade range. The project was to
design a t-shirt that captured the energy of a mountain day — not a
corporate logo on a shirt, but a piece of apparel someone would
actually wear.

## The design

The graphic combines a snow splatter background with illustrated
typography. The splatter gives it motion and texture. The lettering
gives it voice. Together they read as a poster that happens to be on
a shirt.

[[01-tshirt-mockup.jpg]]

The graphic was built in Illustrator as vector art so it could scale
to any garment size without losing edge quality. The mockup was
assembled in Photoshop, with the graphic placed on a real shirt
photograph to check scale and placement.

[[02-tshirt-graphic.png]]

## The components

The illustrated typography is the anchor. Every letter is hand-drawn —
not a font, not a filter. The strokes have weight variation and
character that a set typeface couldn't deliver.

[[04-illustration-typography.png]]

The snow splatter sits behind the type. It's a textured layer built
from custom brushes, giving the whole piece a sense of speed and
impact that a flat background wouldn't carry.

[[05-snow-splatter.png]]

The full design brings both together — the splatter, the type, and
the Stevens Pass brand mark — into a single graphic that works as a
chest print or a full-front design.
