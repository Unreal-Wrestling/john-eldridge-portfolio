Arisen From Nothing were a rock band out of the Pacific Northwest. I
worked with them from 2012 through 2016 under my own studio, Inkboard
Design — shooting their live shows, running press and promo sessions,
designing their logo and the sleeve for their EP, and mixing two tracks
on that record.

Most of it was unpaid. I took it on to build a body of music-industry
work and to open doors with other bands, with the idea that it might
lead to a larger record design project. That never materialised. What it
did produce was four years of steady access to a working band, which
turned out to be its own kind of training.

Two pieces of artwork recur in these photographs that I did not make.
The band already had an engraved crest — Surrectum Ex Nihilum, In
Nothing We Trust — applied to the bass drum head and an amp cabinet
before I arrived. The skull-and-revolvers emblem on the EP front was
supplied to me to build around. The photographs are mine and the sleeve
layout is mine. Those two marks are theirs.

## Live Music Photography

Six venues across four years: Hempfest, Hard Rock Cafe, Studio 7,
Flight's, a firefighter benefit, and Evergreen.

Outdoor festival daylight at one end of the range, small rooms running
whatever the house rig happened to be at the other. Access varied just
as much — sometimes a pit, sometimes wherever there was space to stand.
The job never changed: work with the light that is already in the room,
because you do not get to adjust it and you do not get a second take.

## Design & Mixing

The logo saw more use than anything else I made for them. The bass
player already used an anarchy symbol, so I built the wordmark around it
instead of replacing it — ARISEN FROM NOTHING set in a spray-spatter
treatment with the anarchy A leading the line. I drew it in black and
white so it would sit on either background.

[[afn-logo.png]]

The EP came in 2014. I designed the sleeve as a single wrap, front and
back panel together: the tracklist, the credits, the ghosted song titles
running behind the type, and the cracked ground the whole thing sits on.
The emblem at the centre was supplied by the band and I built the layout
around it. Inkboard Design is credited on the back panel.

I also mixed and mastered two tracks on that release — the Indigo Mix
versions of Bring the War and In Nothing We Trust. TJ Hill recorded,
mixed and mastered the rest of the record.

[[ep-sleeve-01.jpg]]

For social media I built a banner around a treated close-up, running the
wordmark across it with the band's seal set into the corner.

I also designed show posters for them across the same stretch of years,
including a Hard Rock Cafe date and a 2013 tour poster.
