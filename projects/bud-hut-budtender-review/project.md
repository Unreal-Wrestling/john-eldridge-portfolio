Bud Hut is a cannabis retail chain with locations across Washington
state. I worked there at the time, and part of my role was handling
design needs — everything from store signage to internal operations
documents. The budtender QC review sheet was one of those internal
projects.

The store managers needed a standardized way to evaluate their sales
associates. Quality control at a cannabis retail counter covers a
lot of ground: product knowledge, compliance with state
regulations, customer service, cash handling, and store
presentation. The managers had been using informal notes and
inconsistent checklists. I was asked to design a single review sheet
that would standardize the process across all locations.

I started by creating a QC logo — a small circular badge that would
sit at the top of the sheet and brand the review process as its own
thing, separate from the Bud Hut retail identity. The logo gave the
document a sense of authority. This wasn't a manager's notebook
scribble — it was an official evaluation tool.

The first version of the review sheet laid out all the evaluation
criteria across a single page with rating fields and space for
notes. It worked, but the layout was dense. Managers feedback was
that it felt cramped and some of the sections ran together. I
revised the layout in a second version, condensing some criteria,
improving the spacing between sections, and repositioning the QC
logo for cleaner printing. The second version is the one that went
into regular use.

The final artwork was delivered as print-ready InDesign files with
PDF exports for immediate distribution to store managers.
