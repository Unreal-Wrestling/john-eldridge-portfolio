Blank You Very Much was a contest-based platform that connected major
brands with creatives. Brands posted a brief — apparel designs,
packaging concepts, brand collateral — and designers submitted work
for a shot at the contract. It was speculative by design: you were
competing against a national field, the brand picked a winner, and
everyone else walked away with portfolio pieces and a ranking.

I entered four contests between August 2014 and April 2015. None of
them won. I placed highly in all of them. The work speaks for
itself — each contest was a different brand, a different medium, and
a different design problem, and the submissions held their own
against a competitive field.

## Asphalt Yacht Club

Asphalt Yacht Club was a streetwear brand with a nautical-meets-
asphalt aesthetic. The contest brief called for apparel designs —
tee shirts and related graphics that fit the brand's identity. I
submitted seven designs, each working within the existing visual
language while pushing the nautical and motorsport references in
different directions.

## Grenco G Pen Pro

Grenco Science made the G Pen Pro, a dry herb vaporizer. The contest
brief was for packaging and product concept designs — box cuts,
retail-ready packaging, and visual concepts that positioned the
product for a lifestyle market. I submitted three packaging designs
and three concept pieces, working across Illustrator and Photoshop
to produce print-ready dieline files and presentation mockups.

## Modernica

Modernica is a Los Angeles furniture manufacturer known for the
fiberglass Eames shell chair. The contest brief called for brand
designs that connected the furniture to a broader lifestyle context.
I submitted two designs, each using photography and graphic treatment
to position the brand beyond the product itself.

## Red Bull Global Rallycross

Red Bull Global Rallycross was a motorsport series. The contest brief
called for apparel designs — tee shirts for the racing series that
could sell at events and online. I submitted four shirt designs plus
a rally course map graphic. The shirt designs worked across
Illustrator for vector illustration and Photoshop for mockup
presentation; the map was built in Illustrator as a stylized course
diagram.
