Advent Antiquity was a book design project for Graph 251 at Everett
Community College. The assignment was to design a complete book from
cover to closing page — build the grid, choose the type, set the pages,
and prep it for print-on-demand through Blurb. I built it around my own
concept: a fictional band called Advent Antiquity and their album
*Nothing is Trivial*, exploring the idea that music is God.

The name ties directly into that idea. "Advent" is the arrival of the
divine — the moment music enters the world and transforms silence.
"Antiquity" makes it timeless, ancient, a force humans have always used
to connect with the sublime. And if music is sacred, then every note,
lyric, photograph, and design choice in the book has meaning. Nothing
is trivial.

## The cover

The cover sets the tone for everything inside it. The title is set in a
high-contrast serif with tight tracking, sitting in a field of negative
space. I worked through several black-and-white iterations before
committing to color.

## The spreads

The interior runs on a modular grid — a twelve-column structure that
gives every spread a consistent rhythm without making any two pages
look the same. Full-bleed photographs carry across the gutter. Body
text sits in the margin where the image leaves room, and pull quotes
break out at twice the leading to give the eye a rest.

[[grid:03-spread-photo:wide]]

Section openers run a full-bleed image on the left page with the
section title set into the negative space. The right page picks up the
body text on the grid.

[[05-spread-chapter.png]]

The grid holds the insert together, but the photography does the heavy
lifting. I selected images that could carry a spread on their own and
let the typography stay quiet around them.

[[grid:06-spread-fullbleed:wide]]

[[07-spread-grid.png]]

The closing spread mirrors the opener — a full-bleed image on the left,
a single quiet page of type on the right.

[[08-spread-closing.png]]

## Standalone pieces

Two spreads broke from the main flow. "Breathe" is a pure typography
exercise — one word, set at full page height, with nothing else on the
page. "On My Own" pairs an illustration with hand-set type, working as
a self-contained visual statement.

[[grid:09-breathe:med]]

[[10-on-my-own.png]]

The whole book was built in InDesign — the grid, the master pages,
the paragraph styles, the object styles, the preflight. The final PDF
went to Blurb's trade paperback specifications.
