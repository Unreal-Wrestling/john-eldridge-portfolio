Advent Antiquity was a self-directed book design project for Graph 251 at
Everett Community College. The brief was open: design a complete book from
cover to closing page, build the grid, choose the type, set the pages, and
prep it for print-on-demand through Blurb.

## The cover

I started with the cover because it sets the tone for everything inside it.
The title is set in a high-contrast serif with tight tracking, sitting in
a field of negative space. I worked through several black-and-white
iterations before committing to color.

## The spreads

The interior runs on a modular grid — a twelve-column structure that gives
every spread a consistent rhythm without making any two pages look the
same. Full-bleed photographs carry across the gutter. Body text sits in
the margin where the image leaves room, and pull quotes break out at twice
the leading to give the eye a rest.

[[grid:03-spread-photo:wide]]

Chapter openers run a full-bleed image on the left page with the chapter
title set into the negative space. The right page picks up the body text
on the grid.

[[05-spread-chapter.png]]

The grid holds the book together, but the photography does the heavy
lifting. I selected images that could carry a spread on their own and
let the typography stay quiet around them.

[[grid:06-spread-fullbleed:wide]]

[[07-spread-grid.png]]

The closing spread mirrors the opener — a full-bleed image on the left,
a single quiet page of type on the right.

[[08-spread-closing.png]]

## Standalone pieces

Two spreads broke from the main flow. "Breathe" is a pure typography
exercise — one word, set at full page height, with nothing else on the
page. "On My Own" pairs an illustration with hand-set type, working as
a self-contained visual statement.

[[grid:09-breathe:med]]

[[10-on-my-own.png]]

The whole book was built in InDesign — the grid, the master pages, the
paragraph styles, the object styles, the preflight. The final PDF went
to Blurb's trade paperback specifications.
