Advent Antiquity was a book design project for Graph 251 at Everett
Community College. The assignment was to design a complete book from
cover to closing page — build the grid, choose the type, set the pages,
and prep it for print-on-demand through Blurb. I built it around my own
concept: a fictional band called Advent Antiquity and their album
*Nothing is Trivial*. The band's name holds the spiritual layer —
"Advent" is the arrival of the divine, the moment music enters the
world and transforms silence. "Antiquity" makes it timeless, ancient,
a force humans have always used to connect with the sublime.

The album itself is about something more grounded. *Nothing is Trivial*
is about navigating the challenges of life and still working to be a
whole and decent human being. The challenges are part of the learning,
and the destination wouldn't be the same without them. Every note,
lyric, photograph, and design choice in the book carries that weight —
nothing is trivial.

## The cover

The front cover sets the tone for everything inside it. All of the
title work throughout the book — the cover, the section openers, the
song titles — was created with bamboo brushes and india ink, then
digitized.

## The interior

The interior was composed spread by spread. The front matter and the
chapter openers hold to a fixed structure — the margins, the type sizes,
and the placement of the red chapter numerals repeat, so the book has a
spine you can feel as you move through it. The song spreads were set
visually inside those rules, one at a time, which is why no two of them
are laid out the same way. The lyrics carry the voice of the book, set
against traditional artwork converted into digital artwork, full-bleed
photography, typography, and hand-lettered titles. Hailey Francis
modelled for most of the photography in the book.

## Production

The whole book was built in InDesign — the master pages, the paragraph
styles, the object styles, the preflight. The final PDF went to Blurb's
trade paperback specifications.

This project is where everything I had learned to that point came
together in one place: creative writing, photography, digital
illustration, typography, layout, and graphic design. Every lesson went
into it.
