These four projects came out of Graph 110 and Graph 113 at Everett
Community College, two classes that covered the full range of what
Illustrator can do — from precise pen-tool drafting to gradient mesh
painting to original letterform design.

## Movie poster

The poster was the capstone for Graph 110. A complete one-sheet — title
treatment, supporting imagery, billing block, the whole layout. I built
it as a print-ready file at one-sheet dimensions.

[[01-movie-poster.jpg]]

## Gradient mesh

The mesh project was the hardest piece in Graph 113. Gradient mesh is
Illustrator's closest thing to painting — you lay a grid of points over
a shape and assign a color to each one, and the tool interpolates
everything in between. The result reads as a photorealistic render, but
it's entirely vector.

[[02-gradient-mesh.png]]

## Technical illustration

The airplane was a precision exercise. Every line is a pen-tool path,
every curve is a deliberate Bezier. No live trace, no shortcuts. The
goal was to see how far clean vector drafting could carry a complex
mechanical subject.

[[03-airplane.png]]

## The V Project

The V Project was a study in using type to create an image. White V's
on a black background form the Guy Fawkes mask in negative space — the
face, the mustache, the goatee, every feature is a V placed at a
specific size and angle. A few parentheses sneak into the goatee and
eyebrow, but it's 99% the letter V. The hat and hair are shaped from
V's iconic speech in the film, and the main text is his introduction
speech. The piece won me a spot in the Everett Community College
Vibrations Magazine for an artist spotlight.
