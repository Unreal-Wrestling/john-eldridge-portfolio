Flights Pub was a bar in Everett with an aviation theme — the name
was a nod to the city's Boeing heritage and Paine Field. The owner
needed a logo, some merch, a show poster, and help with the website.
It was the kind of small-client work that covers a little bit of
everything.

The logo came first. The concept was a paper airplane forming the
crossbar of the "F" in "Flights" — a mark that read as both
aviation and a bar napkin folded into a plane. It was simple enough
to work at any size, from a social media avatar to a sign on the
wall. The final logo was built as a vector in Illustrator and
delivered as an AI file and PNG.

The T-shirt mockups tested two directions: black and burnt orange.
Black was the stronger option — the logo popped against it, and it
worked as both staff uniform and merch for sale. The orange was
warmer and more on-theme for an aviation bar, but the contrast
wasn't as strong. Black went into production.

The show poster was for a Polecat birthday show on January 24,
2014. Polecat was a local band, and the bill included Witchburn and
other acts. The poster needed to fit the stacked concert poster
format — band names, date, venue, door time — while looking like
something someone would actually want on their wall. I incorporated
the band logos into the layout and kept the Flights Pub branding
in the footer.

The web design was a two-phase project. Phase one was the bid and
initial build — site architecture, navigation structure, and
content layout. I delivered wireframes and a working layout
screenshot in the spring of 2013. Phase two was a follow-up scope
for additional pages and functionality. The bid documents and
completed work records are in the project file, but the screenshot
shows where the design landed.
